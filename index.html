Curated Questions to Ask Every Vendor

  1. What is included in the quoted price, and what is extra?
  2. How many weddings have you served at our venue / in our style?
  3. Do you have a backup plan for illness, weather, or emergencies?
  4. What is your deposit, payment schedule, and refund policy?
  5. Do you carry liability insurance? May I see proof?
  6. What time will you arrive and depart? Setup and breakdown?
  7. Do you provide a vendor meal? Is that an extra cost?
  8. Who is the on-site lead the day-of? What if they cancel?
  9. Can we see a full event from start to finish (not just highlights)?
  10. Are you comfortable with a Christian ceremony / Scripture / hymns?

Start Planning / Introduction

Welcome - Start Here

This page gives you one clear flow: protect your work with backups, know what the planner cannot do offline, then follow the live planning path.

This planner runs entirely on your computer. Everything you type saves automatically to a private SQLite database in this browser, with a localStorage mirror for extra crash safety. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is saved to a cloud account unless you download a backup yourself.

If you clear browser history, cookies, or site data — or open this file on another device or browser without restoring a backup — your saved planner data will not be there. Use Download Backup regularly to save a portable .sqlite file. Wait a few seconds after opening the planner before your first backup so SQLite finishes loading.

New users start in Essentials View — a calmer menu focused on setup, guests, budget, and timeline. Switch to Full planner anytime from Profile & Display.

This planner cannot send invitations, RSVP reminders, or vendor emails. Use Email Templates to copy ready-made text into your own mail app, Share Packets for handoffs, and partner backup files when someone else needs to help edit.

Planning With Your Partner (Offline)

No cloud account is required. The main editor protects the plan with Download Backup (.sqlite), shares lightweight updates through a partner sync packet, and uses Share Packets for vendor, wedding-party, and coordinator handoffs that auto-fill from your planner.

Partner workflow: main editor downloads backup → partner restores on their device → partner sends a fresh .sqlite backup back. Partner packets merge guest RSVP updates and new tasks — they do not delete your existing data. Share Packets are best when someone only needs read-ready details, not full edit access.

How the Planner Connects

The pages share information so you do not have to enter the same details twice.

1

Setup personalizes the planner

Your names, date, venues, budget, and time zone feed the Dashboard, countdown, calendar, and guided next-step logic.

2

Budget pages stay connected

Payments feed Budget. Catering & Menu can create managed budget lines. Contracts can connect to payment records, and linked-record views show what is attached.

3

People details flow outward

Guest List feeds Table Layout, catering headcounts, meal counts, Smart Calendar, share packets, and wedding-party planning.

4

Dates gather in Smart Calendar

Tasks, appointments, payments, planning timeline items, and calendar entries can appear together in Month View, Week View, and Agenda View. Use the separate Appointments page for the full appointment tracker.

5

Data Health checks connections

The Dashboard flags missing links like a payment without a vendor, a guest on a deleted table, or a calendar item whose source needs review.

6

Faith and handoff pages support the plan

Prayer Journal, Scripture & Prayer, Premarital Counseling, packets, notes, and Marriage Rhythms help keep the wedding and newlywed transition Christ-centered.

Every table has row checkboxes for bulk edits. Long text boxes wrap and can be dragged taller. The Dashboard updates automatically, and the FAQ page answers common questions anytime.

Backup & Restore Details

The guided path will remind you to protect the planner. This section explains exactly what saving, backup, and restore mean.

Save Your Work

Everything you type saves automatically to a private SQLite database in this browser, with a localStorage mirror for crash safety. On first open, a Protect your planning welcome may invite you to download your first backup. A gentle reminder banner can also appear after many edits or when a backup is overdue.

Download Backup Regularly

Click Download Backup in the top bar to save a complete .sqlite backup file to your computer. If backup says it is still loading, wait a few seconds after opening the planner for SQLite to finish booting, then try again. You can also use Export CSV to download any list (guests, budget, payments, and more) as a spreadsheet.

Restore If Needed

To restore on this device or a new one, click Restore and select your backup file. Restore accepts .sqlite, .db, and legacy .json backups from earlier versions.

Keep your backup files somewhere safe, like a folder on your desktop or cloud storage (Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox, etc.).

UI / UX

UX Principles

The interface is designed around a small set of usability and visual-design principles. Each screen is checked against user intent — for the single page, for the flow between pages, and for the planner as a whole.

PrincipleWhat it meansHow the planner applies it
Hierarchy & EmphasisMost important thing is seen first.One primary action per view; countdown hero and "Needs you" card lead the Dashboard.
Aesthetic–UsabilityClean design feels easier to use.Editorial type, hairline rules, generous white space, squared cards.
Hick's & Miller's LawFewer choices, grouped in small sets.Category nav, "More" overflow, cards grouped in rows of 3; no long flat menus.
Fitts's LawTargets are large and reachable.44px minimum tap targets; full-width primary CTAs; thumb-zone buttons on mobile.
Proximity & Common RegionRelated items sit together in a container.Bento cards, tables, and reconciliation panels group related data.
Alignment & GridElements align to a shared grid.12-column grid, consistent gutters, tabular-aligned figures.
Contrast & ColorEmphasis through weight and hue, accessibly.Theme tokens (forest/gold/burgundy) with a theme-independent dark mode.
Repetition & UnityConsistent, reusable patterns.Shared components: mastheads, stat bands, cards, status dots, tokens.
Zeigarnik & Goal-GradientUnfinished tasks pull attention; progress motivates.Setup checklist shows only what's left; progress bars and % complete.
Jakob's LawMatch familiar conventions.Regular month-grid calendar, breadcrumb wayfinding, standard form patterns.

Reference sets: Laws of UX and the core graphic-design principles (alignment, contrast, balance, hierarchy, color, white space, proportion, repetition, rhythm, movement, emphasis, proximity, unity).

Architecture

Shared systems

Every page composes from these cross-cutting systems. The unification goal is that each recurring pattern is owned by exactly one of them; overlaps flagged below are what Phase 2 consolidates.

Foundation

  • Design tokens — planner-tokens.css: gutter, spacing, radii, hairlines, surfaces, type, --dm-* dark palette.
  • Theming — THEMES, applyTheme(), theme tokens (--forest, --gold, --theme-primary).
  • Dark mode — applyDarkMode(), body.dark-mode, --dm-* remap (theme-independent).

Page chrome

  • Masthead — injectMasthead() + PAGE_HELP / PAGE_EYEBROW / PAGE_ORDER.
  • Scripture / prompts / help — injectPageScripture, injectPrayerPrompt, injectPageHelp, renderPageUxChrome, context strip, goal bars.

Components

  • Editorial set (ued-*) — page, panel, mast, band/stat, table-card/head, btn/link, progress, caption (+ helpers uedStat, uedCaption, uedIcon).
  • Records & status — hub-record-card, status pills, .ro-badge-inline.

Tables & data editing

  • CWP engine — cwpRenderTable, CWP.TABLES, .cwp-section/.cwp-toolbar/.cwp-btn, .ro-preview, header merge.
  • Data Hub — DATA_HUB_REGISTRY, openDataHub.
  • Record editor — openRecordEditor, inline shells, shared modal.
  • Table utilities — enhanceAllTables, resize/auto-fit, currency normalize.

Navigation & shell

  • NAV_CATEGORIES, category bar, showPanel(), context sidebar, Quick Jump, favorites/recents, top bar, footer.

Data & persistence

  • SQLite layer — sqlite-init, db-repository, sqlite-backup, migrations; data + save(); localStorage profiles.
  • Cross-page sync — sync*ToBudget; Smart Calendar aggregator (buildSmartCalendarEvents).

Print / export

  • Covenant print templates (openCovenantPrintTemplate, cvp* skins, printCurrentPage, printAllCovenantSheets), CSV import/export.

Guidance

  • Guided-setup coach, ambient coach pill, next-steps path.
