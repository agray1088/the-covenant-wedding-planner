Planning Timeline — visual directions

One screen, four treatments. 1a is today's page recreated from your source files as the baseline. 1b, 1c and 1d are the three directions — all share the same fixes you asked for (top-level tabs with sub-nav, a top bar reduced to search + account, one button system, one spacing scale) and differ in visual language and primary work surface. Reference an id in chat and I'll take it forward into the full spec.

1a
Baseline — today's screen
Recreated from index.html, planner.css and planner-shell.css. Twelve controls in the top bar, eleven uppercase category menus wrapping to two rows, a 67px display title on a data page, four equal-weight outline buttons, a seven-item stat strip, and a 288px context sidebar duplicating stats already on the page. Below the fold the real page continues with phase suggestion chips and an overall-progress card — both cut off here by the 1020px frame.
Left as it is on purpose. This is the record of what the app looks like today, so its navigation is the old model — 11 categories, “Calendar”, Notes under Planning. Directions 1b–1d have been updated to the final §06 model; this one has not, because a baseline that quietly adopts the new system stops being a baseline.
