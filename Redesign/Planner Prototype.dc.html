What is real and what is not. The six pages share one store, and every number in a stat strip, rail meter, cost line or budget row is computed from it — there are no typed-in totals anywhere. What is deliberately absent: adding and deleting records, the filter chips beyond the RSVP filter, printing, and the other thirty-seven pages. Those are drawn in Planner Screens, and this prototype exists to prove the wiring underneath them rather than to replace them.
