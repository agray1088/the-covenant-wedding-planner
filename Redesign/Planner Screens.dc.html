Budget logic
How these numbers are produced
Planned is what you set per category — either a target percentage of the total budget or a fixed dollar amount. Percentages recalculate when the total changes; fixed amounts don't.
Spent is budget entries plus synced payments, and a category is only ever counted in one of those two columns. The moment a payment record is linked to a category, that category’s money moves wholly into Payments and its manual entries stop counting — so nothing double-counts. Photography and the Emergency fund are the only manual categories here; the other six are payment-backed.
Category owners — Catering & Menu owns Food, Cake, Drinks and Rentals; Payments owns anything with a contract. Those rows are read-only here and edited on the page that owns them.
Gratuity is the sum of the ticked Tipping Etiquette rows — $1,000. Where a vendor record already carries a gratuity (Grace Hall $250, Adom Sound $200) that is the same money, counted once here, not added twice.
Variance is planned minus spent, so negative means over target. The Emergency fund is excluded from the True Total until you spend from it.
