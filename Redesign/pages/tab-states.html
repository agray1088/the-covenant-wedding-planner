One file per tab state across the fifteen pages that split themselves. Each carries the app’s own data-tab values and handler names — cerTab, hmTab, entTab, visionTab, vndTab — so the existing JavaScript works untouched.

Three of the fifteen turned out not to need a tab strip at all. Gifts became two labelled chip groups, Smart Calendar and Wedding Day became view switchers. Those are marked below, and the reasoning is in Planner Sub-Tabs and §07b of the spec.
